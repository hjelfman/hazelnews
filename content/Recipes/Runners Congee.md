Ingredients 
- 1/4 cup rice uncooked
- 2 tsp psyllium husk
- 3 tbsp aspertame 
- 4 cups water
- 4 tbsp cocoa powder
- 1 tbsp cinnamon 
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract
- salt

Steps
1. Pressure cook rice in water on high for 45 minutes
2. Natural release, then set to sautee
3. Add all other ingredients aside from aspertame while stirring vigorously 
4. Cut the heat and add aspertame while stirring
5. Pour into bowl at desired consistency
